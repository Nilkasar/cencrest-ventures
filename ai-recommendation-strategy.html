AI Recommendation Strategy

Turn the findings into results.

The AI Recommendation Strategy takes the findings from your AI Visibility Audit and converts them into an executed content, authority and technical programme — with measurable improvements to your AI Visibility Score.

Get In Touch → Start with an Audit

Three levers that move AI recommendations.

AI models recommend brands they have seen cited, described and corroborated — across multiple sources they trust. Improving AI Visibility means improving three things: what exists about you, where it exists, and whether a crawler can read it.

LEVER 01 · CONTENT
Close the retrievability gap

Facts that are true about your business but not published in a form AI can retrieve. We identify the specific missing claims and help you publish them in HTML, in the right format, on the right domains.

LEVER 02 · AUTHORITY
Build third-party corroboration

AI models trust third-party evidence more than owned content. We identify where your competitors have built citation authority — reviews, trade press, expert mentions, structured data — and build a programme to close the gap.

LEVER 03 · TECHNICAL
Fix what crawlers cannot read

Client-rendered pages, noindex directives, PDF-only content and JavaScript-gated information are invisible to AI crawlers. We audit and fix every technical barrier that prevents AI models from reading your content.

LEVER 04 · STRUCTURE
Speak in the language AI retrieves

AI models prefer structured, specific, factual content. We review your web presence for schema markup, semantic HTML, answer-formatted content, and AI-readable structure — and upgrade what needs to change.

The programme.

The AI Recommendation Strategy is not a one-time project. It is a 90-day programme designed to produce measurable improvements to your AI Visibility Score, with monthly checkpoints and a final score comparison to your baseline.

  • Month 1 · Foundation. Fix technical crawlability issues. Publish the highest-priority missing content. Add schema markup and semantic structure where absent.
  • Month 2 · Authority. Build third-party citation authority — trade press placements, review platform presence, expert attributions, and structured corroboration.
  • Month 3 · Consolidation. Close remaining content gaps. Optimize what has been published for AI retrievability. Prepare for ongoing monitoring.
  • Score check. At the end of the programme, we re-run all prompts across all four models and compare your new AI Visibility Score to your baseline.
