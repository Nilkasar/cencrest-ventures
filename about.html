Evidence over opinion.

Most AI marketing advice is guesswork. We believe in measurement. Everything we do starts with running the actual prompts your buyers use, across all four major AI models, and recording what comes back. No assumptions. No proxy metrics. Direct evidence.

We then trace those answers back to their sources — the specific websites, articles, reviews and datasets that the models are reading and trusting. That is where the gap is. That is where the opportunity is.

Founded on a simple observation.

BeBest was founded by Nilesh after observing a consistent pattern: businesses that were genuinely better than their competitors were being systematically overlooked by AI assistants — not because they lacked merit, but because the evidence for their merit was not in a form that AI could retrieve.

The companies winning in AI recommendations were not necessarily the best products. They were the best documented, best cited, and most retrievably described. That gap between quality and AI-perceived quality is what BeBest was built to close.

What we stand for.

  • Measurement before opinion. We run the prompts before we make any claim about your AI Visibility. Data drives every recommendation.
  • Specificity over generality. We name the exact sources, gaps and actions — not broad themes and vague advice.
  • Retrievability over volume. More content is not always the answer. The right content, in the right place, in a form AI can read, is.
  • Corroboration over claims. AI models trust third-party evidence more than brand-owned content. Our strategy reflects that reality.
  • Transparency over mystique. We show you exactly what we found, how we found it, and what to do about it.
