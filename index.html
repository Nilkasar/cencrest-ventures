Common Questions

Everything about AI Visibility.

What is AI Visibility?

AI Visibility is a measure of how often and how prominently your brand appears in responses from AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity when potential buyers ask questions in your category. As more buyers use AI to research vendors, your AI Visibility determines whether your brand is in the conversation at all.

How is an AI Visibility Score calculated?

Your AI Visibility Score is calculated by running 1,400+ buying prompts across four major AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity — and measuring how frequently and prominently your brand is mentioned compared to category competitors. The score reflects your current share of AI-generated recommendations.

Which AI models do you analyze?

We analyze four leading AI assistants: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), and Perplexity. These represent the AI models most commonly used by B2B buyers for research and vendor discovery. Each model has different retrieval patterns, which is why measuring across all four matters.

What is the AI Visibility Snapshot and is it really free?

Yes. The AI Visibility Snapshot is a completely free assessment that shows you how AI assistants currently perceive and recommend your brand. It includes an initial AI Visibility Score, a competitor comparison, and a summary of your current recommendation frequency. There is no obligation and no credit card required — just complete the form and we will be in touch within 24 hours.

How long does a full AI Visibility Audit take?

A full AI Visibility Audit typically takes 2–3 weeks from kickoff. This includes running all prompts across four models, analyzing citation patterns and source data, mapping competitor visibility, and compiling the full AI Brand Intelligence Report with your action plan.

How is BeBest different from an SEO agency?

BeBest focuses on AI-driven discovery and recommendation, not traditional search engine rankings. While SEO improves Google visibility, AI Visibility Intelligence improves how AI assistants learn, trust and recommend your brand — which requires understanding how models retrieve and use content, not just how they rank pages. The signals that move AI recommendations are fundamentally different from those that move Google rankings.

Can AI Visibility actually be improved?

Yes. Once we identify why AI models currently overlook or underrepresent your brand — whether due to content gaps, citation deficits, crawlability issues or authority signals — we develop a targeted action plan to systematically improve your AI recommendation frequency. Clients who implement recommendations typically see measurable score improvements within 60–90 days.

What is the AI Brand Intelligence Report?

The AI Brand Intelligence Report is BeBest's comprehensive deliverable. It documents your AI Visibility Score across all four models, maps every competitor citation and source, identifies your specific content and trust gaps, and lists the exact actions required to become the brand AI recommends in your category. It is designed to be a working document your team can act on immediately.
