Key Concepts

CONCEPT
What is AI Visibility?

AI Visibility is a measure of how often and how prominently your brand appears in AI assistant responses when buyers ask questions in your category. See our full explanation on the homepage.

CONCEPT
What is an AI Visibility Score?

A percentage score reflecting your share of AI-generated recommendations across 1,400+ buying prompts and four AI models. Industry leaders average 68%. Most businesses start below 10%.

CONCEPT
What drives AI recommendations?

Three factors: retrievable content (information the model can read), third-party corroboration (citations from sources the model trusts), and technical accessibility (pages the crawler can actually index).

CONCEPT
How is this different from SEO?

SEO targets Google's ranking algorithm. AI Visibility targets how language models retrieve and use content — which responds to different signals. A high-ranking page may still be invisible to AI.
