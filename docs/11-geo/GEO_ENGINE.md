# GEO ENGINE — BeBest (Generative Engine Optimization)

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-11

## PURPOSE

The BeBest GEO Engine measures, analyzes, and improves how AI assistants discover, understand, and recommend a brand. It operates across 4 AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) and produces evidence-backed observations about brand visibility, competitive position, and content gaps.

This is ENGINE 2 of BeBest's two growth engines.

---

## WHAT THE GEO ENGINE MEASURES

### Brand Dimensions
| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| AI Visibility Score | Overall score (0–100) of how frequently and prominently the brand appears across all tested queries and models |
| Mention Rate | % of queries where the brand is mentioned at all |
| Recommendation Rate | % of queries where the brand is recommended as a top choice |
| Position Score | Weighted average of where in responses the brand appears (1st mention = highest score) |
| Sentiment Score | Distribution of positive / neutral / negative mentions |
| Category Association | Which categories AI associates this brand with |
| Entity Association | Which entities AI co-mentions with this brand |
| Citation Score | How often the brand's own domain appears as a cited source |

### Competitor Dimensions
Same as brand dimensions, applied to each configured competitor. Enables:
- Competitive visibility comparison
- Share of AI voice
- Gap analysis

### Intent Dimensions
For each intent (buying question):
- How many models mention the brand?
- What position does the brand appear?
- Which competitors appear instead?
- What content do models reference?
- What claims do models make?

## AI VISIBILITY SCORE — FORMULA v1.0

The AI Visibility Score (AVS) is a composite deterministic score.

### Components

**Mention Score (MS)**: 
```
MS = (mentions across all queries and models) / (total queries × total models) × 100
```

**Recommendation Score (RS)**:
```
RS = (queries where brand is top-3 recommendation) / (total queries) × 100
```

**Position Score (PS)**:
```
For each mention, position_weight = 1 / mention_position
PS = average(position_weight) × 100
```

**Coverage Score (CS)**:
```
CS = (intents covered by brand) / (total intents in query universe) × 100
```

**Composite AVS**:
```
AVS = (MS × 0.25) + (RS × 0.40) + (PS × 0.20) + (CS × 0.15)
```

**Formula version**: 1.0 — stored with every computed score  
**Changes to formula**: require version increment and re-computation of historical scores with change log

## QUERY UNIVERSE

The foundation of the GEO engine is the **Query Universe** — the set of buying questions that real customers ask AI assistants.

### Query Categories
For every brand analyzed, BeBest generates queries across:

1. **Category queries** — "What is [category]?"
2. **Problem queries** — "How do I solve [problem this category solves]?"
3. **Commercial queries** — "Best [category] for [use case]?"
4. **Comparison queries** — "[Brand] vs [Competitor]"
5. **Feature queries** — "Which [category] has [specific feature]?"
6. **Industry queries** — "Best [category] for [industry]?"
7. **Size queries** — "Best [category] for [company size]?"
8. **Geography queries** — "Best [category] in [location]?" (where relevant)
9. **Intent queries** — "How to [specific job-to-be-done]?"
10. **Authority queries** — "Who are the experts in [category]?"

### Query Generation
Queries are generated by the Brand Intelligence module + AI-assisted expansion:
1. Seed queries from brand profile (categories, use cases, products)
2. Competitor-informed expansion (what questions do competitors appear in?)
3. AI-assisted elaboration (generate variants of each seed query)
4. Human review + curation (for paid tiers)

### Query Universe Size
| Tier | Queries per Baseline |
|---|---|
| Free Snapshot | 20–50 sample queries |
| Starter | 200 queries |
| Growth | 500 queries |
| Pro | 1,400+ queries |
| Enterprise | Custom (5,000+) |

## AI RESPONSE PROCESSING

### What is Stored
Every AI response stores:

```json
{
  "id": "uuid",
  "run_id": "uuid",
  "query_id": "uuid",
  "provider": "anthropic",
  "model": "claude-opus-4-5",
  "prompt_version": "1.2",
  "temperature": 0.7,
  "raw_response": "full response text...",
  "response_word_count": 342,
  "latency_ms": 1240,
  "tokens_used": 512,
  "timestamp": "2026-08-11T12:00:00Z",
  "observations": {
    "brand_mentioned": true,
    "brand_position": 2,
    "brand_sentiment": "positive",
    "brand_context": "described as ideal for mid-market companies",
    "competitors_mentioned": ["CompetitorA", "CompetitorB"],
    "citations": ["g2.com/...", "freightwaves.com/..."],
    "recommendation_made": true,
    "recommendation_strength": "strong"
  },
  "extraction_model": "qwen3:8b",
  "extraction_prompt_version": "1.0",
  "scoring_formula_version": "1.0"
}
```

### Observation Extraction Pipeline

1. **Raw response received** from AI provider
2. **Preprocessing**: strip excessive whitespace, detect language, check length
3. **Brand mention detection**: is the brand name (or alias) present? (deterministic string match + LLM fallback for paraphrases)
4. **Position extraction**: where in the response does the first mention occur? (character offset → normalized 0–1)
5. **Sentiment extraction**: LLM extracts sentiment around the brand mention (with chain-of-thought)
6. **Competitor extraction**: which competitor names appear?
7. **Citation extraction**: which URLs are cited or referenced?
8. **Recommendation detection**: does the response recommend a product? Is the brand recommended?
9. **Claim extraction**: what factual claims does the response make about the brand?
10. **Score computation**: deterministic formulas applied to extracted observations
11. **Storage**: raw response + observations + scores stored with full traceability

### Evidence Traceability
Every score must be explainable:
```
AI Visibility Score: 32/100

Breakdown:
  Mention Rate: 41% of queries → Mention Score: 41
  Recommendation Rate: 28% of queries → Recommendation Score: 28
  Avg. Position Score: 0.48 → Position Score: 48
  Intent Coverage: 19% → Coverage Score: 19

Formula: (41 × 0.25) + (28 × 0.40) + (48 × 0.20) + (19 × 0.15) = 32
Formula Version: 1.0
Run ID: [uuid] — click to see raw responses
```

## COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE IN GEO

For each competitor:
- Run the same query universe
- Extract the same observations
- Compute the same scores

**Competitive Gap** = Competitor AVS − Your AVS

**Share of AI Voice** = Your mention count / (Your mentions + all competitor mentions) × 100

### Competitor Tracking
- Competitors defined in Brand Intelligence profile
- Up to N competitors per plan tier
- Per-intent competitor analysis: "For the query 'best freight visibility software,' CompetitorA appears in 84% of responses at position 1. You appear in 2%."

## GEO GAP ANALYSIS

### Gap Types

**1. Intent Gaps** — Queries where competitors appear but the brand does not
```
Intent: "managed X hosting for enterprise"
Competitor A: appears in 90% of responses
Competitor B: appears in 76% of responses  
Your brand: appears in 0% of responses
→ HIGH PRIORITY GAP
```

**2. Content Gaps** — Topics AI discusses that the brand has no authoritative content for
```
AI frequently cites "G2 reviews showing X brand has fastest onboarding"
Your brand: no G2 reviews, no onboarding benchmark published
→ CONTENT/AUTHORITY GAP
```

**3. Entity Gaps** — AI categories or entities the brand is NOT associated with
```
AI associates Category X with: CompetitorA, CompetitorB, CompetitorC
Your brand: not associated with Category X despite serving it
→ ENTITY ASSOCIATION GAP
```

**4. Source Gaps** — Trusted domains AI cites that contain no brand content
```
g2.com: cited in 31% of responses. Your brand: 11 reviews.
Industry blog: cited in 28% of responses. Your brand: not mentioned.
→ AUTHORITY/CITATION GAP
```

## GEO OPTIMIZATION PRINCIPLES

These are the evidence-backed levers for improving AI visibility:

1. **Publish machine-readable content** — AI cannot retrieve what it cannot read. Server-render key pages. No JS-only content.

2. **Establish entity associations** — Create content that clearly associates your brand with your category, use cases, and differentiators. AI must learn what you do.

3. **Build authoritative citations** — Appear on the domains AI already trusts: G2, industry publications, comparison sites, analyst reports.

4. **Publish named evidence** — Named benchmarks, methodologies, and data points become citable facts. "Company X's 2025 study showed..." is citable. Generic marketing copy is not.

5. **Structure claims for retrieval** — Write content in statement form AI can lift directly. FAQ format, numbered lists, and quoted expert opinions are highly citable.

6. **Cover every commercial intent** — Build dedicated pages for every use case, industry, and comparison. AI maps topics to pages.

7. **Maintain content freshness** — AI models are updated. Stale content falls out of the model's trusted set.

8. **Verified authority signals** — Review volume, named experts, published data, industry recognition — these are what AI uses as credibility signals.

## GEO AGENT (See Epic 17)

The GEO Agent automates the GEO intelligence loop:

1. **Profile** — understand brand, competitors, categories
2. **Generate** — create query universe for the brand's category
3. **Run** — execute queries across all configured AI providers
4. **Extract** — parse responses for brand mentions, citations, competitors
5. **Score** — compute AI Visibility Score (deterministic)
6. **Diagnose** — identify gaps and opportunities
7. **Recommend** — generate specific, evidence-backed recommendations
8. **Measure** — re-run after changes, compare scores, attribute improvement
