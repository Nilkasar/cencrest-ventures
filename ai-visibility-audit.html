The AI Visibility Audit.

A comprehensive, evidence-based analysis of how AI assistants discover, trust and recommend your business — delivered as a full AI Brand Intelligence Report with a prioritized action plan.

Request an Audit → Start with a Free Snapshot

What the Audit covers.

The AI Visibility Audit runs 1,400+ buying prompts — the actual questions your buyers ask AI assistants when researching solutions in your category — across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Every response is recorded, analyzed and traced back to its source.

The result is a complete picture of your current AI Visibility: where you stand, why you are being overlooked, who is winning, and exactly what is driving their advantage.

01 · VISIBILITY SCORE
AI Visibility Score

Your overall recommendation frequency across all four AI models, broken down by model and by prompt type. Benchmarked against your category.

02 · CITATION MAPPING
Citation Source Mapping

Every website, publication and source that AI models are reading to form recommendations in your category — ranked by influence and citation frequency.

03 · COMPETITOR ANALYSIS
Competitor AI Presence

Exactly which competitors appear in AI answers, how often, in which models, and why — including the specific sentences that AI models lift and re-use.

04 · CONTENT GAPS
Content & Trust Gap Audit

Facts that are true about your business but not retrievable by AI — missing content, content behind login, PDFs without HTML equivalents, and unpublished claims.

05 · TECHNICAL AUDIT
AI Crawlability Audit

Pages that AI crawlers cannot read — client-rendered content, noindex directives, JavaScript-only pages, and robots.txt exclusions that hurt your AI visibility.

06 · ACTION PLAN
AI Recommendation Action Plan

A prioritized list of specific moves — ranked by effort and impact — to increase your recommendation frequency. Everything is specific, named and actionable.

The deliverable: AI Brand Intelligence Report.

The AI Brand Intelligence Report is a comprehensive written document your team can read, act on and share. It is not a slide deck of observations — it is a working document with named sources, quantified gaps, and specific instructions.

  • AI Visibility Score with model-by-model breakdown and category benchmark
  • Citation source map with domain authority and citation frequency for each source
  • Competitor presence analysis with verbatim AI answer excerpts
  • Content gap register with publication priority and recommended format for each gap
  • Technical crawlability findings with specific pages and recommended fixes
  • Prioritized action plan with effort/impact matrix for each recommendation

Timeline and process.

Week 1 · Kickoff and prompt design

We work with you to confirm your target category, buyer personas and key competitors. We then design and validate the 1,400+ prompt set that will be run across all four models.

Week 2 · Prompt execution and source tracing

All prompts are run across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Every response is recorded. Every citation source is identified and analyzed. Competitor patterns are mapped.

Week 3 · Analysis and report writing

We synthesize all findings into the AI Brand Intelligence Report. The action plan is prioritized by impact and effort. Every recommendation is specific and named.

Delivery · Presentation and handoff

We present the findings and walk through the action plan with your team. The full report is yours to keep and act on immediately, with or without continued engagement.

FAQ

Questions about the Audit.

Who is the AI Visibility Audit for?

The Audit is designed for B2B companies that rely on discovery and word-of-mouth in their category — particularly those where buyers use AI to research and shortlist vendors. It is most valuable for companies with 10+ competitors and buyers who ask research questions before engaging sales.

Do I need to start with the free Snapshot?

No. You can go directly to the Audit. The free AI Visibility Snapshot is available for companies that want to understand their baseline before committing to a full audit. Many clients start with the Snapshot and upgrade immediately.

What information do you need from us?

We need your company name, website, primary product or service category, target buyer persona, and a list of your top 5–10 competitors. Everything else — the prompts, the analysis, the source tracing — we handle ourselves.

Can you help us implement the action plan after the Audit?

Yes. See our AI Recommendation Strategy service for implementation support. We can also set up AI Visibility Monitoring to track your progress after implementation.
