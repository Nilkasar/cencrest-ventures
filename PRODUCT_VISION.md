## THE ONE-LINE VISION

> "BeBest is the AI + SEO Growth Autopilot that tells businesses exactly what to do next to be discovered in search and recommended by AI — then helps them do it."

---

## THE PROBLEM

When buyers have a question, they ask AI assistants. When they search, they use Google. In both cases, the same thing happens: **a small number of brands dominate, and everyone else is invisible.**

The brands that dominate are not necessarily the best. They are the most **retrievable** — they have:
- The right content that AI can read and trust
- The right authority signals AI can verify
- The right citation patterns from sources AI already trusts
- The right entity associations in AI's world model

Most companies have no idea why competitors get recommended instead of them. They cannot measure it. They cannot diagnose it. They cannot fix it without guessing.

---

## THE SOLUTION

BeBest runs 1,400+ buying questions through 4 AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity). It maps every citation, scores every mention, and traces every recommendation to its source.

Then it does the same for traditional search.

Then it combines both signals into a unified view:

> "Here is what buyers are searching for. Here is who AI recommends. Here is why. Here is what to do about it."

## PRODUCT PILLARS

### 1. AI Recommendation Intelligence (GEO)
We measure exactly how AI assistants see, understand, and recommend every brand in a category. Not aggregate trends — specific evidence per query, per model, per brand, per date.

### 2. Search Growth Intelligence (SEO)
We identify what buyers search for, where the brand ranks, what competitors rank for, and where the highest-value content opportunities exist.

### 3. Unified Opportunity Engine
We combine SEO demand signals with GEO visibility gaps to identify the opportunities that are simultaneously valuable in search AND invisible in AI — the highest-ROI intervention points.

### 4. Evidence-Backed Recommendations
Every recommendation traces to evidence. "Build a managed hosting comparison page" is not a guess — it is backed by:
- 1,200 monthly searches for "managed X hosting"
- Competitor X appearing in AI answers for this query 84% of the time
- Your brand appearing 2% of the time
- Your website having no page on this topic

### 5. Human-Approved Execution
The system generates; humans approve; the system executes. Autonomy is earned through demonstrated accuracy, not assumed.

### 6. Continuous Measurement
Every change is an experiment. Every experiment is measured. The system learns what works in this specific customer's category and adjusts its recommendations accordingly.

## AUTONOMY LEVELS

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recommend | System identifies opportunities and recommends actions. Human decides and implements. |
| 2 | Draft | System drafts content, metadata, structured data. Human reviews and implements. |
| 3 | Approve & Execute | System drafts + presents for one-click approval. Human approves; system publishes. |
| 4 | Autonomous | System acts within pre-approved parameters. Human reviews results. |

Every customer starts at Level 1. Levels 3–4 require explicit opt-in and audit trails.

Autonomy levels NEVER apply to:
- Billing or account changes
- Security or credential changes
- Account deletion
- Unapproved external communication
- Any action marked HIGH risk
