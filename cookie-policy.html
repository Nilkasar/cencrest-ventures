Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remaining on your device for a set period).

How BeBest uses cookies

bebestwithai.com is a primarily static website with minimal third-party dependencies. We aim to minimize cookie use. The cookies we use fall into the following categories:

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They do not store any personally identifiable information. Examples include cookies that remember your cookie consent preference.

Font and resource loading

We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google may set cookies or collect limited technical data as part of this service. We have added crossorigin attributes and preconnect hints to minimize data transfer. Google's use of font data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics cookies

We do not currently use Google Analytics or other third-party analytics platforms that set tracking cookies. If we introduce analytics in future, we will update this policy and, where required, seek your consent before setting analytics cookies.

Third-party cookies

We do not embed third-party widgets, social media buttons, or advertising networks that would set cookies without your knowledge. If this changes, we will update this policy.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device
  • Delete all cookies or specific cookies
  • Block all cookies or cookies from specific sites
  • Set preferences for how cookies are handled

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website. To manage cookies in your browser, refer to your browser's help documentation.

Your consent

By continuing to use bebestwithai.com, you consent to the use of strictly necessary cookies as described in this policy. Where we use any non-essential cookies in future, we will seek your explicit consent before doing so.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised date.
